# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> **In short:** everyone taking part treats others with respect; unacceptable behaviour is reported privately to the maintainer, who may correct, warn, suspend or ban.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community through respectful, tolerant and kind communication.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention, trolling or insulting and derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without explicit permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project community spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour should be reported privately to the project maintainer using the private contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue to report conduct involving private or sensitive information.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers responsible for enforcement must respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use the Contributor Covenant Community Impact Guidelines when determining consequences:

1. **Correction** — a private written warning for inappropriate or unprofessional behaviour, with an explanation of why it was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour and, where appropriate, a defined period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary Ban** — a temporary ban from project interaction for a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour.
4. **Permanent Ban** — permanent exclusion from project interaction for a pattern of violations, harassment, aggression or disparagement.
